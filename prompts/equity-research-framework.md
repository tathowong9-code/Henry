# Equity Research Framework (3-part prompt set)

Replace `$[TICKER]` and run each part in order. Each part needs live web data.

---

## Part 1 — Catalyst Breakdown (<300 words)

You are an institutional equity research analyst. For ticker $[TICKER], give me a complete catalyst breakdown.

Search the web for current data. Cover these in order:

1. **CURRENT SETUP**
   - Price right now, market cap, 30-day and YTD performance
   - The recent move that put this stock on people's radar
2. **THE DOMINANT NARRATIVE**
   - What is the bull thesis being repeated on X / Reddit / TikTok?
   - Quote the most viral framing if there is one
   - Note any specific price targets retail is calling for
3. **THE ACTUAL CATALYST**
   - The real event that triggered the move (earnings, contract, partnership, policy change). Be specific with dates and dollar amounts
   - What did the company actually announce or report?
   - What did management say about forward guidance?
4. **THE INSTITUTIONAL VIEW**
   - Recent analyst target changes (upgrades, downgrades, reiterations)
   - Where consensus sits vs current price
   - Any institutional buying or selling (13F changes, insider activity)

End with: *"The stock is moving because [REAL CATALYST], but [WHAT NOBODY IS TALKING ABOUT] is the part that matters."*

Keep response under 300 words. Be specific. No vague language.

---

## Part 2 — Financial Health & Competitive Valuation

You are an institutional equity research analyst. For ticker $[TICKER], do a financial health and competitive valuation breakdown.

1. **THE STOCK ITSELF**: price, market cap, 30-day/YTD; forward P/E and EV/Sales; last-quarter revenue growth, EPS growth, gross margin; cash, debt, YoY share-count change (dilution); last management guidance (revenue and EPS).
2. **THE 5 CLOSEST COMPETITORS**: table with ticker/name, forward P/E, forward EV/Sales, last-quarter revenue growth, market cap, one-line positioning note.
3. **THE VERDICT**: premium / in line / discount — show the math; flag any anomaly.

End with: *"If you believe [BULL THESIS], $[TICKER] is [CHEAP / FAIR / EXPENSIVE] versus its peers because [REASON]."*

---

## Part 3 — Forward Framework: Sentiment, Analysts, Scenarios, Trade Plan

You are an institutional equity research analyst. For ticker $[TICKER], build a complete forward-looking framework.

1. **RETAIL SENTIMENT**: Yahoo Finance conversation tone; 2–3 top bullish and bearish takes from X/StockTwits; is sentiment uniform (warning sign) or split?
2. **ANALYST PROJECTIONS**: analyst count, consensus rating, 12-month target (median/high/low), price vs consensus, last-30-day target changes, FY revenue & EPS estimates for next 1–2 years.
3. **FOUR PRICE SCENARIOS** (with math): Bear (3–6 mo), Base (6–12 mo), Bull (12–18 mo), Stretched Bull (24 mo).
4. **THE TRADE PLAN**: entry zone, first trim (25–50%), second trim, hard stop.

End with: *"This is a [TRADE / INVESTMENT / PASS] because [REASON]."*
