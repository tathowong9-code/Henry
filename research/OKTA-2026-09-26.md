## PART 1 — Catalyst Breakdown

**1. CURRENT SETUP**
- $196.50 · market cap ≈ $36B · YTD ≈ +142%
- Since the Aug 26 pre-print close (≈ $134), the stock is up ≈ +46%.
- It jumped 19% after the Aug 26 earnings report, rallied again on Sep 14 when AI-safety warnings lifted security stocks, and slipped 3.4% after Oktane on Sep 25.

**2. DOMINANT NARRATIVE**
- Bull thesis: *"Okta prices by the seat, and AI agents are the next seat."* The idea is that every AI agent needs an identity, and Okta can bill for each one.
- The Oktane 2026 launches are the talking points: Agent SSO, an AI-agent runtime gateway and an "AI kill switch."
- Retail targets: $230–240, from the new Street highs.

**3. ACTUAL CATALYST (Aug 26, Q2 FY27)**
- Revenue: $805M (+11%). Subscription revenue: $793M (+12%).
- Non-GAAP EPS: $1.05 vs a $0.97 estimate.
- RPO grew 17% and cRPO grew 14%, an acceleration of ≈ 200bp. **Management said the cRPO acceleration came from execution, not AI.**
- Free cash flow: $227M, a 28% margin.
- FY27 guidance: revenue $3.216–3.226B (+10–11%), EPS $3.90–3.94, FCF $910–930M. This is the second raise this year.

**4. INSTITUTIONAL VIEW**
- Target changes after Oktane: DA Davidson $235, Roth $235, Wells Fargo $230, RBC $230, Needham $200→$230, Guggenheim $227, BofA $200→$220 (Neutral), Stifel $215, TD Cowen $175→$200 (Hold).
- Consensus (34 analysts): Strong Buy, but the average target of **$187 is below the current price**.
- Okta bought back 1.5M shares for $125M in Q2; $555M remains authorized.

**Verdict:** *The stock is moving because cRPO accelerated to 14% and guidance was raised twice, but the multiple more than doubling while revenue growth stayed at 11% is the part that matters.*

### 3. Verdict — the math
- **EV/Sales:** 10.5x vs a peer median of 22x = **≈ 52% discount**.
- **Growth-adjusted (EV/Sales ÷ growth):** OKTA 10.5/11 = **0.95** vs a peer median of 0.65 = **≈ 46% premium**.
- **P/E:** 50x vs a peer median of ≈ 90x = ≈ 44% discount. However, ZS grows 2.3x faster at a lower P/E (46x).
- **Anomaly:** The stock rerated from ≈ 4x to ≈ 10.5x EV/Sales this year, yet revenue growth only moved from ≈ 10% to 11%. **All of the +142% is multiple expansion, not earnings.**

> **If you believe agentic identity reaccelerates revenue growth to mid-teens by FY28, $OKTA is FAIR versus its peers, because a 28% FCF margin justifies about 10x sales. Without reacceleration, it is EXPENSIVE, because it trades at 0.95x sales per point of growth against ZS at 0.34x.**
