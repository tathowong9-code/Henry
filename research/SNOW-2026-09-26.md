# $SNOW — Snowflake Inc. | Equity Research Pack

**As of:** September 26, 2026 · **Price:** $336.75 · **Ticker selected because:** biggest large-cap software beat-and-raise of the September print cycle (17% one-day gain, 13+ target raises in 48 hours), and the stock has since given back roughly two-thirds of that jump. That pullback is what makes it worth a look now.

## PART 1 — Catalyst Breakdown

**1. CURRENT SETUP**
- $336.75 · market cap ≈ $117.7B · YTD ≈ +52% · vs. Sep 2 pre-print close ($319.87): +5.3%
- Closed Sep 3 at $356.56 (+17%, 4th-largest move since IPO); now 5.6% below that close.

**2. DOMINANT NARRATIVE**
- Bull thesis: *"AI fears flipped."* AI agents drive more data consumption, and that data sits in Snowflake.
- Most-shared framing (Wall St Engine on X): *"Revenue $1.55B (Est. $1.48B) 🟢 … Adj. EPS $0.62 (Est. $0.45) 🟢 … Raises FY27 Guide."* StockTwits sentiment is rated "extremely bullish," and message volume rose 2,500%.
- Retail anchors to **$500** (UBS) and the Street-high **$525**. I found no verified retail-specific target.

**3. ACTUAL CATALYST (Sep 2, Q2 FY27)**
- Revenue: $1.55B (+35%). Product revenue: $1.49B (+37%, the third straight quarter of acceleration).
- RPO: $9.0B (+30%). NRR: 126%. Customers above $1M: 828 (+27%).
- The CoCo AI coding agent added 2,000+ accounts, for 9,100 total. Management says AI drove about half of the acceleration.
- Guidance: FY27 product revenue raised to **$6.07B from $5.84B** (+36%). Non-GAAP operating margin raised to 14.5% from 13.5%. Q3 product revenue guided to $1.588–1.593B (+37–38%).

**4. INSTITUTIONAL VIEW**
- Target raises: MS $300→$470, UBS $425→$500, GS $300→$436, JPM $285→$426, Baird $296→$450, Argus $300→$450.
- Consensus is Buy, with an average target of ≈$425–434, which is about +27% from here.
- 13F (as of Jun 30): 103 hedge funds hold it, up from 80. Point72 raised its position 2,449% to $716M and Citadel raised 117% to $510M. Viking and Maverick opened new positions.
- CEO Form 4s (Sep 8/15/21) are tax withholding on vesting RSUs, not open-market sales.

**Verdict:** *The stock is moving because product revenue re-accelerated to 37% and FY27 guidance was raised $230M, but the AI mix pulling non-GAAP product gross margin guidance down to 74% is the part that matters.*

*Private comp: Databricks, with a ≈ $5.4B run-rate growing 65%. It is a head-to-head substitute, and a possible IPO around $134B would give the market a direct public comparison.*

### 3. Verdict — the math
- **Raw EV/Sales:** SNOW 18.3x vs peer median 10.6x (MDB) = **~73% premium**.
- **Growth-adjusted (EV/Sales ÷ growth):** SNOW 0.52 vs DDOG 0.57, PLTR 0.59, MDB 0.35, ESTC 0.29. The median of those four is 0.46, so SNOW is at a **~13% premium**. That is roughly in line with its peer group.
- **Anomaly:** SNOW trades *cheaper* than DDOG on sales (18.3x vs 20.5x) with *faster* growth (37% vs 36%, and 37–38% guided vs 28–29%). It is also 75% *more expensive* on earnings (167x vs 95x). The whole gap comes from margins: a 15% non-GAAP operating margin and a gross margin that is shrinking.

> **If you believe AI workloads keep product growth ≥35% through FY28 *and* operating margin scales past 20%, $SNOW is FAIR-to-CHEAP versus its peers, because it is growing faster than DDOG at a lower sales multiple. If margins stall at the mid-teens, it is EXPENSIVE, because at 167x forward earnings it is priced like PLTR without PLTR's profitability.**
